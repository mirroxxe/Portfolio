## ESSENCIAL — Redefining Premium Fashion & Architectural Apparel

**ESSENCIAL** is an exclusive, high-end digital flagship store created for a premium fashion brand specializing in tailored, architectural silhouettes and sustainable luxury garments. Merging high-fashion editorial aesthetics (inspired by Balenciaga and modern avant-garde houses) with strict UI clarity, the platform presents high-value items through an elevated, immersive, and distraction-free digital environment.

**The Core Challenge:** The primary challenge was to strike a delicate balance between high-end editorial storytelling and seamless functional e-commerce UX. High-ticket fashion items require an atmospheric, minimalist visual presentation with ample whitespace, precise grid alignment, and crisp typography. At the same time, the shopping experience needed to remain intuitive, ensuring that catalog filtration, item sizing, user profile management, and multi-item checkout are fast and frictionless.

**My Role & Solution:** I designed a complete digital ecosystem for the brand, focusing on three key structural pillars:

- **Editorial Visual Language & Grid System:** Designed a stark, modern layout anchored by high-contrast serif/sans-serif typography, subtle border lines, and full-width imagery that places garment texture and craftsmanship front and center.
- **Streamlined Catalog & Product Page Architecture:** Created a highly functional product listing page with multi-parameter filtering (category, size, material) and a detailed product page complete with "Complete the Look" cross-selling modules, precise size charts, and quick "Add to Cart" triggers.
- **Comprehensive User Ecosystem & Loyalty Hub:** Built an intuitive authentication flow, a structured shopping cart with promo code engine and cross-recommendations, and a personal dashboard featuring real-time order tracking and the "ESSENCIAL Club" loyalty tier progress.

**The Ultimate Goal:** The objective was to transform the online purchasing of high-value garments into an effortless art-gallery experience, maintaining the sophisticated standards of luxury fashion while maximizing conversion rates across every step of the funnel.

## Target Audience

- **Connoisseurs of High Fashion**: Discriminating buyers who seek understated luxury, clean architectural cuts, and high-quality natural materials.
- **Modern Minimalists**: Style-conscious consumers who value clean, clutter-free interfaces and expect an online shopping experience that matches the exclusivity of a boutique.
- **VIP & Club Members**: Loyal brand patrons who actively track their order history, take advantage of personal club discounts, and engage with early collection drops.

## Workflow

- **Information Architecture & Wireframing**: Mapping out the complete buyer journey from hero landing banners and catalog navigation to product detail views, cart summary, and user dashboard states.
- **Design System & Component Library**: Establishing strict typographic hierarchies, input field states, status badges (e.g., in transit, delivered), subtle grid dividers, and modular product cards.
- **High-Fidelity Desktop & Mobile Prototyping**: Crafting fully interactive layouts in Figma to refine edge cases, including interactive auth overlays, size chart popups, multi-item cart recalculations, and loyalty program progress bars.
