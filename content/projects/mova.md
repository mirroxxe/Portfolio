## MOVA — Real-Time Translated Messaging & Voice Assistant

**MOVA** is an innovative AI-powered messaging platform designed to remove language barriers in global communication. By automatically translating text messages, voice notes, and live audio calls into the user's native language in real time, the application allows people worldwide to connect effortlessly without needing a shared language.

**The Core Challenge:** The main challenge was to design a seamless, non-intrusive UI that presents both original and translated text simultaneously without cluttering the chat screen. Additionally, for audio calls and voice messages, the app needed to deliver real-time voice synthesis and caption overlays with clear visual indicators for ongoing translations.

**My Role & Solution:** I designed a complete mobile messaging experience focusing on three main pillars:

- **Dual-Language Chat Interface:** Clean message bubble hierarchy showing the translated message prominently alongside the option to view the original source text.
- **Live Call & Voice Translation:** An immersive call screen with live auto-captions and real-time audio translation indicators for cross-border voice calls.
- **Smart Onboarding & Location Attachments:** Simple onboarding flow for quick native-language selection and interactive features like location sharing, media attachment, and contact management.

**The Ultimate Goal:** The objective was to make international communication as simple and natural as texting a friend in your home language, backed by high-fidelity UI and intuitive gesture controls.

## Target Audience

- **Global Professionals & Remote Teams**: Individuals communicating daily with international partners, clients, or colleagues across different languages.
- **Travelers & Expats**: Users living or traveling abroad who need instant, reliable text and voice translation for everyday social interactions.
- **Multilingual Families & Communities**: Friends and relatives speaking different native languages who want to stay connected effortlessly.

## Workflow

- **User Journey & Interaction Mapping**: Mapping out user flows from quick onboarding and language setup to messaging, call translation, and attachment sharing.
- **Component & Design System**: Developing a cohesive design system with soft gradients, dark mode contrast, clear language badges, and responsive UI elements.
- **High-Fidelity Mobile Prototyping**: Crafting detailed Figma screens covering core chat threads, incoming call interfaces, overlay modals, and attachment drawers.
