## VRTX — Minimalist Modern Fashion & Smart Shopping Companion

**VRTX** is a next-generation fashion e-commerce mobile application designed to redefine how modern consumers discover, style, and purchase curated apparel. Built around a clean, timeless aesthetic with bold typographic hierarchy and intuitive touch interactions, VRTX merges high-end streetwear and minimal apparel with intelligent, personalized shopping tools.

**The Core Challenge:** The primary challenge was to balance a refined, minimalist aesthetic with rich functional utility. Traditional fashion apps often overwhelm users with fragmented filters, complex size guides, or intrusive customer support systems. VRTX needed to simplify the product discovery path, eliminate hesitation during size selection, and integrate an interactive styling companion without cluttering the sleek visual language.

**My Role & Solution:** I designed a comprehensive mobile ecosystem focused on three key areas:

- **Streamlined Discovery & Checkout:** An intuitive navigation flow including an ultra-clear Home feed, interactive filter drawer, single-tap cart management, and a friction-free checkout path.
- **Smart Outfit Builder & Fit Assistance:** An AI-powered recommendations engine (“AI Outfit Builder”) that suggests complete looks around selected pieces, paired with an accessible interactive Size Guide & Fit Advice tool.
- **Integrated Customer Touchpoints:** Real-time Support Chat and cohesive empty/error states (404 Page) that maintain brand voice and aesthetic alignment across every interaction.

**The Ultimate Goal:** The objective was to make high-end apparel shopping as seamless and enjoyable as browsing a digital lookbook, combining smart personalization with an effortless user journey.

## Target Audience

- **Fashion-Forward Minimalists**: Style-conscious buyers looking for curated, high-quality garments with an emphasis on clean design and timeless looks.
- **Mobile-First Shoppers**: Digital natives who demand fast navigation, immediate support, and precise size recommendations right from their phones.
- **Tech-Savvy Consumers**: Users who value smart features like AI-driven styling advice and personalized total look recommendations.

## Workflow

- **Information Architecture & Wireframing**: Structuring core user paths from product discovery and filtering to checkout, support, and AI-assisted outfit building.
- **Visual Design & Grid System**: Establishing a robust layout grid, clean typographic scale, and a sophisticated color palette anchored by warm neutrals and bold burgundy accents.
- **High-Fidelity Prototyping & Edge Cases**: Designing full interactive screens in Figma, including critical secondary states such as full-screen photo galleries, size guide modals, custom 404 views, and live chat overlays.
